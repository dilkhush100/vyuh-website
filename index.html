Smart OBD-II Diagnostics

Vehicle Tech

Transform any car into a smart vehicle. Plugs directly into your car's OBD-II port to provide real-time health monitoring, predictive maintenance, and instant alerts straight to your phone.

  • Predictive Maintenance: AI algorithms detect potential engine or sensor failures before they become expensive breakdowns.
  • Real-time Alerts: Instant smartphone notifications for engine fault codes, low battery, and abnormal temperatures.
  • Plug & Play: Zero wiring required. Installs in seconds under your dashboard.

Intelligent Water Automation

Smart Home

Never miss a municipal water supply cycle again. This smart controller actively monitors incoming water lines and automatically manages your pump to ensure optimal storage without wastage.

  • Supply Detection: High-precision sensors instantly detect the arrival of municipal water pressure.
  • Auto Pump Operation: Automatically turns the motor ON when supply arrives and OFF when tanks are full or supply stops.
  • Dry-Run Protection: Prevents motor damage by shutting down if the water flow is interrupted.
